Who we approve

Audience size matters less than whether your audience believes you. A small engaged following in research, laboratory or scientific circles is worth more to us than a large general one, and we approve on that basis.

What partners can and cannot say

NovaFlex supplies research-use-only material, and that is the basis on which the business operates. We hold partners to the same standard we hold ourselves, which is why the last line below is the one that matters: if we could not say it on our own site, please don't say it carrying our code. We would rather set that out before you apply than after you have made something.

  • Assayed purity and how it was measured
  • Third-party testing, and who did it
  • Certificates of analysis, matched per lot
  • Sourcing, shipping and turnaround
  • Pricing and your discount code
  • How to evaluate any supplier's paperwork
  • Anything presented as medical advice
  • Directions telling your audience to use the material
  • Claims we could not put on our own site

Questions

How much does it pay?

Commission is set per partner and runs up to 25% of the order total, uncapped. The rate depends on your audience and where you publish. Everyone who uses your code gets a discount too, so the code is worth sharing on its own merits rather than being a pure ask.

Does it cost anything, and is it exclusive?

No fee, no minimum sales requirement, and no exclusivity. Keep working with whoever else you work with.

Do I need a big following?

No. We would rather have five hundred people who trust you than fifty thousand who scroll past. Approval is based on fit and credibility, not follower count.

How do payouts work?

Commission accrues on every paid order placed with your code and appears in your partner dashboard in real time, itemised per order so the total is auditable. Payouts are issued on request once a balance has accrued.

What exactly can I say about the products?

Supply facts are always safe ground — purity, testing, certificates, sourcing, shipping and price. Beyond that the standard is simple: if we could not put it on our own site, please don't put it out carrying our code. Nothing framed as medical advice, and nothing that reads as directions telling your audience to use the material. The same constraint governs our own marketing, so we are not asking anything of partners we do not do ourselves.

I'm already a partner. Where do I log in?

Your dashboard is at the partner portal, where you can grab your code and track commission.
