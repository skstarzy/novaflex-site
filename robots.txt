# AI training / scraping / answer-engine crawlers are disallowed below.
# Anthropic's Claude bots are explicitly ALLOWED.
# Search engines (Googlebot, Bingbot, etc.) and payment / brand-protection /
# compliance scanners are NOT restricted here — they fall under "User-agent: *".
#
# NOTE: robots.txt is advisory. Reputable crawlers honor it; malicious scanners
# ignore it. Real enforcement is handled at the Cloudflare edge (WAF rules).

# Allowed — RETRIEVAL crawlers.

# now; they are not training corpora. "Which suppliers actually publish a COA"

# an assistant rather than typed into a search box. Blocking these removed
# NovaFlex from that answer entirely while protecting nothing — the training
# crawlers below are the ones that take without giving back, and they stay
# blocked.
